Privacy Policy - ROSA-BET PRO

Last Updated: Oct 15, 2026 | App: ROSA-BET PRO - Five AIs. One verdict.

Your Data Stays Yours. Always.

At ROSA-BET, your privacy comes first. Unlike other betting apps, we:
  • We don't collect personal match data (no contacts, location, tracking)
  • Record offline, view when connected - like ScoreCastPro
  • Store everything locally - your settings stay on your device
  • Never sell your data - our model is built on transparency, not ads

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect any personally identifiable information. Like scorecastpro.ai, we collect only:

  • Usage Data: crash logs, performance metrics (via Render.com) - anonymous
  • Third-Party: Public sports odds from The Odds API (no personal info)
  • Your club deserves an app that's secure, respectful, and high-performance - no compromises.

2. How We Use AI Data (5 Models)

Our app shows predictions from 5 AIs: ROSA PRO proprietary engine (90% accuracy claim) + ChatGPT + Claude + Gemini + Grok side by side, as described on scorecastpro.ai. Data used only to display:

  • AI Confidence (e.g., 82%)
  • Live xG 2.34 +0.18 last 5 min
  • AI Consensus 4/5 Home Win
  • Match Result, Over/Under 2.5, BTTS, xG differential

3. Third-Party Services

  • The Odds API - live odds, public data
  • Render.com - hosts rosa-bet-server
  • GitHub Pages - hosts ROSA-BET-APP
  • scorecastpro.ai - inspiration for Five AIs model

4. Data Security

HTTPS everywhere. No login, no password, no personal data stored. We do not use your information for advertising profiling.

5. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Contractual necessity - enabling core App functions. Legitimate interests - improving stability. Consent - optional communications. You can clear cache anytime.
